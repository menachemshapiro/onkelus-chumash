How to use this page

Each pasuk is split into short clauses. Each clause shows the Hebrew, Targum Onkelos and the English side by side (stacked on a phone), so you can see which Aramaic words translate which Hebrew words. Rashi follows each pasuk.

Getting around

  • Use the pickers at the top to choose a sefer, parsha, aliyah, perek or pasuk.
  • Type a reference in the small box to jump to it, e.g. 32:13, לב יג or Vayechi 49:11.

What to show

  • Te'amim, Targum, English, Rashi turn each part on or off.
  • Highlights: where Onkelos departs from a word-for-word translation, the Aramaic is highlighted, and so is the English that explains it.
  • English drop-down: Combined is the Metsudah English with Onkelos's wording in [brackets]. Plain takes the brackets out, so the English follows the Hebrew. Onkelos rewrites the English to follow the Targum, with Onkelos's own wording in bold.
  • Word by word adds an Onkelos word by word drop-down under each line. It shows which English renders each Aramaic word, with short notes where something needs explaining.

The Targum glossary (beta)

  • Tap any Aramaic word for a card with its parts (prefixes, the word, endings), root, grammatical form, meaning in this pasuk, and dictionary entry. The glossary is new and will have mistakes.
  • Where the card shows Jastrow, tap Open entry to read the word's entry in Jastrow's dictionary. It was matched automatically, so now and then it may be the wrong entry.

Look and export

  • Aa sets fonts, sizes and layout. Your choices are saved in this browser.
  • ⤓ exports a parsha, aliyah, perek or range of pesukim as a PDF or Word document.

More

  • Show original on a pasuk shows the English as printed, where it was reordered or corrected here.
  • Report on a pasuk or a glossary card sends a note about a mistake.
  • On an iPhone, open the page in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen to use it as an app. It also works offline.
